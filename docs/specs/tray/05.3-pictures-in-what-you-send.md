### 5.3 Pictures on the computer

The agent's side of section 5's row 7: the pictures a message carries. What stays as decided: pictures sent with a message are never stored in D1 or R2. The picture queue and the hub's rate limits are the app's and the cloud's. The agent takes no pictures of the game itself and uploads none: there is no shoot and no picture viewer, and a received `shoot` command is refused as an unknown command (section 9.3 of [agent/09.3](../agent/09.3-what-the-computer-does-when-a-command-asks.md)).

- **Inline in the command.** Every command whose local route took `images` (`answer`, `playtest`, `continue`, `retry`, `ask`, `draft_spec`, `draft_answer`, `draft_revise`) may carry `pictures: [{ type, data }]`: `type` one of `image/png`, `image/jpeg`, `image/gif`, `image/webp`; `data` base64. On a shared backlog the pictures go to the store first and the command carries `pictureIds` instead (last-local-state.md section 3.3). The limits are in `shared/sentPictures.ts`: at most 4 pictures to a message, 1 MiB each decoded, 4 MiB in all.
- **At the agent.** Before it runs one of those commands, `withPictures` saves the pictures with `attachments.saveAll`: the type and size checks (PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, at most 10 MB), a fresh `<uuid>.<ext>` under `.retaskable/attachments/`, pruned after 30 days. All are checked before any is written; one that fails fails the whole command (`nack` with the reason) and nothing is saved. Their paths are then added to the text the session reads (`withImages`), to the notes, the question or the request, or to the last answer.
- **Tested** in `agent/src/commands.test.ts` (both pictures' paths reach the session; `remote.log` holds no bytes; one bad picture saves none).
- **Never kept in the cloud.** `remote.log` records the pictures as `{ count, bytes }` only.
