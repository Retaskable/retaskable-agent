### 6.1 Planner (retired)

Until 2026-10-04 a read-only Opus session (`prompts/planner.md`) turned the backlog file into items whenever its blob changed on `origin/<baseBranch>`, and the orchestrator merged its result into `state.items`, carrying renumbered items over. It is gone ([backlog.md](https://github.com/Retaskable/Retaskable.com/blob/main/docs/specs/backlog.md) section 11): every project's work comes from its backlog, the store's ([backlog.md](https://github.com/Retaskable/Retaskable.com/blob/main/docs/specs/backlog.md) section 8, "Every project is a store project"), which keeps the item list and its dependencies in the backlog store, read by plain code. Its read-only worktree, `<worktreesDir>/_planner`, stays as the project's **base checkout** at `origin/<baseBranch>`, for the baseline (section 6.7), Play main, shoots, phone builds, main syncs and the read-only sessions (chat, triage, release notes, playtest grouping, whose engine and budget still go under the `planner` role).
